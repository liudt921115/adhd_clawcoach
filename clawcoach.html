Core Principles

Built differently,
for different minds.

🤫
Silent when stable
ClawCoach only speaks at state transitions. When you're in flow, it disappears. No unnecessary nudges, no dopamine traps.
🧭
Direction over discipline
The goal stays fixed. The path bends to meet you where you are. Changing method is not failure — it's regulation.
🛡️
Anti-shame architecture
Fail-Safe Reset mode activates after 3 low-execution days. No tracking, no shame, no efficiency talk — just 2-minute tasks until you rebuild.
🪞
Mirror, not motivator
Rewards reflect behavior identity back to you: "You started even in low energy." Not dopamine spikes — recognition of who you're becoming.
🌊
Controlled Drift
Short-term detours are allowed. ClawCoach sets a return time, maintains your main direction, and prevents resistance spirals.
⚡
Urgency interrupt
Before events and meetings, the system calculates preparation time and triggers proactively — solving time blindness before it strikes.
🔬
Intent Check
When resistance appears, ClawCoach asks: do you want to do this, or just feel you should? The answer shapes the path forward, not judgment.
🏠
Routine as foundation
Food, rest, sleep, hygiene — not tasks, but base layer. Sleep Guard and recovery nudges come before productivity.

ClawCoach doesn't do things for you.
It holds you when you're unstable,
pulls you back when you drift,
and quietly disappears when you're steady.
